# u12-articles | Labor Code articles on hours, pay, holidays, leave | Legal Responsibilities › Articles 83 to 95
> Art. 83 Normal hours of work: 8 hours/day; health personnel in cities/municipalities with 1 million+ population: 8 hours/day, 5 days/week OR 40 hours/week, with extra pay beyond; includes public health nurses, government hospital doctors, clinic personnel. Art. 84 Hours worked: time required on duty, time permitted or suffered to work, and short rest breaks controlled by the employer. Art. 86 Night shift differential: 10 PM to 6 AM, at least 10% additional pay per hour. Art. 87 Overtime: beyond 8 hours/day, at least 25% premium on the hourly rate; higher on rest day or holiday (usually 30%+). Art. 88 Undertime cannot be offset by overtime (leaving early today cannot be paid back by overtime tomorrow). Art. 94 Holiday pay: 100% of daily wage even if not working; worked on a regular holiday = 200%; holiday that falls on a rest day and worked = 260%. Art. 95 Service incentive leave: at least 1 year of service = 5 days with pay per year; vacation or sick; unused SIL converted to cash.
