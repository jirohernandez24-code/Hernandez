# u10-standard | Standard; QA vs QI | Standards of Nursing (definition, QA vs QI)
> A standard is a predetermined level of excellence that guides practice. Quality Assurance (QA) = monitoring and evaluating COMPLIANCE with standards (example: a monthly audit of the MAR to check the five rights). Quality Improvement (QI) = PROACTIVE, CONTINUOUS, DATA-DRIVEN change to improve processes (example: introducing an eMAR with staff training and a protocol review).
~ QA = "Are we following the rule?" QI = "How can we make it better?"

:: A standard is set in advance and guides practice.

# u10-scope | Scope of nursing (RA 9173, Art. IV, Sec. 28) | Standards of Nursing › RA 9173 Article IV Section 28

# u10-duties | Duties of nurses, Section 28 (a) to (e) | Standards of Nursing › Section 28 Duties
